# Sergio Torres

**Civil engineer. Builder. Founder of [A DOS ESTUDIO](https://a-dosestudio.com), a design-build studio in Colombia.**

I run construction projects and I build the software that manages them. Around 15 design projects, 6 builds, and 3,200 m² delivered so far.

## What I'm building

**Construction management platform.** An internal tool for my studio: daily site logs, progress tracking by chapter and item, material usage, and a client portal where owners see where their project and their budget stand. In use on my own projects. Private repository.

**Voice-driven site log.** A prototype that turns a field recording into a structured daily report, classified across 13 categories, from progress and materials to safety and change orders.

**Phase-gated design workflow.** A system that takes a project from first client meeting to construction documents, with required checks before each phase.

## Why

On a job site, what happens every day rarely reaches the people making decisions. Budgets slip and the client is the last to know. I build with AI coding tools and test everything on real projects with real clients.
